Prompt 5 · Trackers — the measured life · Margin Notebook system

One visualization per tracker, matched to how you measure it.

Trackers are the — of the notebook: quantities you watch over time. Each has a schema that decides how it's logged and drawn. The dashboard shows the data; one Log button opens an entry dialog matched to that schema — mood and energy you can log many times a day, coffee counts up, weight takes a value, a run is a single tick. Open one and the chart fits the data: a scale gets a year-in-pixels grid, a count gets buckets by week/month/year, a habit gets a streak and heatmap. Everything's live — log, open a tracker, follow any ↳ from your journal back to the bullet it came from.
